## Recording Setup

When recording your own demo:
1. Have test data ready
2. Open all relevant windows (form, CRM, email, logs)
3. Record at 1080p
4. Speak clearly and slowly
5. Pause to let viewers see results
6. Show error handling
7. Upload to Loom or YouTube
